Part 1 · Learn

How the Present Simple works

Six short steps. Read each one, then try the sentence maker at the end.

When do we use it?Qachon ishlatamiz?

Habits and routinesOdatlar va kundalik ishlar

Things we do again and again.

I drink tea every morning.Men har kuni ertalab choy ichaman.

FactsFaktlar

Things that are always true.

Water boils at 100 degrees.Suv 100 darajada qaynaydi.

TimetablesJadvallar

Fixed times for buses, trains and lessons.

The lesson starts at nine.Dars soat to'qqizda boshlanadi.

Positive sentences (+)Darak gaplar

SubjectVerbExample
I · You · We · TheyworkI work in a bank.Men bankda ishlayman.
He · She · ItworksShe works in a bank.U bankda ishlaydi.
Remember: with he, she and it, add -s to the verb.Esda tuting: he, she, it bilan fe'lga -s qo'shiladi.

Negative sentences (−)Inkor gaplar

subject + don't / doesn't + verb

I don't like fish.Men baliqni yoqtirmayman.

She doesn't like fish.U baliqni yoqtirmaydi.

She doesn't likes fish. ✗
She doesn't like fish. ✓
After doesn't, the verb has no -s.doesn't dan keyin fe'lga -s qo'shilmaydi.

Questions (?)So'roq gaplar

Do / Does + subject + verb ?

Do you play tennis?Siz tennis o'ynaysizmi?

Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Does she play tennis?U tennis o'ynaydimi?

Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

Where does he live?U qayerda yashaydi?

What time do you get up?Siz soat nechada turasiz?

After Do or Does, the verb stays in its base form: Does she play, not Does she plays.Do/Does dan keyin ham fe'l asosiy shaklda turadi.

Part 3 · Pronunciation

Shadowing

Shadowing means you speak at the same time as the speaker, like a shadow. It helps your rhythm and your pronunciation.

Start shadowing

youtu.be · opens in a new tab ↗
  1. Listen first.Watch once. Do not speak. Notice the stress and the melody.Bir marta tinglang. Gapirmang. Urgʻu va ohangga eʼtibor bering.
  2. Speak with the voice.Play it again. Repeat each sentence one second after the speaker. Use the same speed.Qayta qoʻying. Har bir gapni gapiruvchidan bir soniya keyin takrorlang.
  3. Repeat three times.Each time, look at the screen less. Try to speak without reading.Har safar ekranga kamroq qarang. Oʻqimasdan gapirishga harakat qiling.
